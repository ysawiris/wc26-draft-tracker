The Draft Board

    The Draw · Groups B–L

    How It Works

    1. 1
      The draw. Each team was randomly assigned a World Cup group, B–L. Group A is out (they already started). One group goes unclaimed.
    2. 2
      Goals decide it. Add up every goal scored by the four nations in your group across the whole group stage. Most goals takes the No. 1 pick.
    3. 3
      Tiebreaker: cards. Level on goals? More cards in your group wins the tie — yellow = +1, red = +2.
    4. 4
      Final order. The ranking sets the official draft order, picks 1 through 10.

    Goals & cards update after each matchday. Disputes go to the commissioner.
